## 📝 USAGE INSTRUCTIONS

### Generate Financial Report
1. Navigate to **Reports** page in Nhyiraba HMS
2. Select **Date Range** (7 days, 30 days, 90 days, 12 months)
3. Choose **Report Type**: Financial
4. Click **Generate PDF Report**
5. Preview the luxury financial report
6. Download or share the PDF

### Report Contents
- Automatically calculates all financial metrics from invoice data
- Displays revenue breakdown by category (Room, F&B, Other)
- Estimates operating expenses (35% of revenue)
- Shows collection rates and pending receivables
- Presents net profit with profit margin percentage
